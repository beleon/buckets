Using buckets with curl

share

curl --data-binary @notes.txt /notes.txt

POST the data to /<filename>; the response is the bucket URL.

fetch

curl /<slug>/notes.txt

Content type is detected at upload and set on delivery. Range and HEAD work.

replace

curl --data-binary @notes.txt /<slug>/notes.txt

Needs the full existing path: slug and filename. Resets the 24 h clock.

delete

curl -X DELETE /<slug>/notes.txt

rules

In the browser: Ctrl+Enter shares the text, pasting an image or dropping a file uploads it.

Filenames: A-Z a-z 0-9 . _ -, max 255 chars, no leading dash.

Limits: 500 MB per upload and in total; oldest buckets are evicted first. 6 uploads per minute.

Everything lives in memory; nothing survives a restart.
